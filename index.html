Takashi Otonari 大隣 嵩

I am currently a second-year PhD student at Yamasaki Lab, the University of Tokyo, specializing in computer vision and graphics. My interests lie in the application of novel view synthesis techniques, such as NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting, particularly in the context of creating 3D content in the real world.
